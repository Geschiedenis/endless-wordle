Word list sources and licences for data/es/answers.json and data/es/valid-guesses.json (Spanish)
================================================================================

These two files are generated by scripts/build-intl-word-lists.mjs from:

1. RLA-ES Spanish dictionary (via the npm package dictionary-es)
   npm package: dictionary-es 4.0.0
   Licence: GPL-3.0, LGPL-3.0 or MPL-1.1, at your choice
   valid-guesses.json is derived from it (stems expanded with its suffix rules), and
   answers.json is a subset of its stems. Its licence notice follows below.

2. FrequencyWords by Hermit Dave, content derived from OpenSubtitles 2018
   https://github.com/hermitdave/FrequencyWords
   Licence: CC BY-SA 4.0 for the content (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/)
   Used only to rank and choose which dictionary words become answers. Because of
   that, answers.json is shared under CC BY-SA 4.0, with attribution to the above.

Words were lowercased, accents were removed except on the letters this language
keeps as separate keys, and words in scripts/blocklists/es.txt were left out of
answers.json.
